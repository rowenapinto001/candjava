# Code Notes Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

Code Notes Lab is an educational app for studying programming concepts and practising code.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores study progress, bookmarks, personal notes, quiz scores, accessibility preferences, daily activity, reminder preferences, and saved playground programs in local app storage. This information is used only to provide the app's study features. Code Notes Lab does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics.

Removing the app or clearing its storage may permanently delete this locally stored information.

## Online code execution

The notes and other reading features work locally. When you choose **Run** or **Run tests** in the playground, the source code and standard input currently shown in the playground are sent over the internet to the Wandbox compilation service so that the program can be compiled and executed. Do not place passwords, personal information, private keys, or confidential source code in the playground.

## Notifications

If you enable a daily study reminder, the app requests notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. The reminder does not require a remote notification server. You can disable it in the app or in Android settings.

## External links

The app may open educational source links in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

## Children's privacy

Code Notes Lab does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not provide accounts, social features, advertising, or behavioural tracking.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will identify the current version.
